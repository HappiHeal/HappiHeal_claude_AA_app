Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the HappiHeal web app available at happiheal.happiloot.com and any future versions on Google Play Store or other platforms.

1. Who We Are

HappiHeal is a digital wellness app created by Harshitha MV, operating under HappiLoot. We help people with alopecia through natural remedies, routines, and holistic guidance based on 20+ years of personal experience.

Contact: contact.happiloot@gmail.com

Wellness tracker only. HappiHeal is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

2. What Data We Collect

Account data: Your name and email address when you sign up. Stored securely through our authentication provider (Supabase).

Payment data: We collect only the payment ID to confirm your purchase and activate your plan. We do not store your card number, UPI ID, or banking details — these are handled entirely by Razorpay under PCI-DSS compliant systems.

App usage data: Your daily logs, streaks, and checklist entries are stored locally on your device only (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it.

Photos: HappiHeal does not ask you to upload photos. Any progress photos you take are saved to your own device gallery — we have no access to them.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No cookies or tracking pixels
  • No health records or medical data
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers
  • No data sold to third parties — ever

We do not process sensitive personal data under India's DPDP framework.

4. How We Use Your Data

Your email is used to: create and manage your account, send plan confirmation, and respond to support requests. We do not send marketing emails without your explicit consent.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and profile storage. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Razorpay — payment processing, PCI-DSS compliant, regulated by RBI-recognized bodies. Razorpay Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Claude API — powers the AI Ask feature. Your questions are sent to the API and are not stored by us. Anthropic Privacy Policy

6. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Email contact.happiloot@gmail.com and we will respond within 7 business days.

7. Data Security & Retention

Your account data is protected using industry-standard security and HTTPS encryption in transit. We follow data minimisation principles and retain your data only while your account is active or as required by law. You may request deletion at any time.

8. Children

HappiHeal is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Significant changes will be communicated via the app or email. Continued use of HappiHeal means you accept the updated policy.
